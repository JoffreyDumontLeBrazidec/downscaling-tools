- 2026-09-09: stage 1 built and complete, including the optional section 5 work. The pairing manifest was read out of the `fake_forecasts` attribute of the AIFS ENS version 2 validtime store and checked against the store's own `dates` array: 446 rows, 223 initialisations, leads 6 and 12 only, and 446 valid times exactly six hours apart from 12 May 2026 18 UTC to 1 September 2026 00 UTC with no gap and no repetition, so section 2 of this note is confirmed as written. Station observations for `2t`, `2d`, `10ff`, `msl` and `tp` at a six-hour period were retrieved from STVL through `vtb.media.stvl_retrieve`, using the same call as `retrieve_observations` in the `obs_crps` backend, in SLURM job 34850096 on the nf queue; `vtb` works on a batch node, so no login-node fallback was needed. The 2,230 requests took 27 minutes 23 seconds and not one of them failed or returned nothing. That produced 26.4 million observation rows over five parquet files and 23,138 distinct stations. The static station table was built in SLURM job 34859566 from the O1280 analysis archive, with the variable indices 10, 25, 31 and 87 re-checked against that store's `variables` attribute before use, and it carries the nearest O1280 point and the twelve nearest with their distances, the model surface height, the sub-grid orography and slope, the land-sea fraction within 15 km, the terrain class, the coastal flag, the region and the station minus model height. The hold-out flag was assigned by hashing the station identifier, taking the sha1 of the identifier modulo 100 and holding out below 5; every terrain class and every region came out within 0.40 percentage points of 5 per cent, so no stratified draw was needed and 1,206 of 23,138 stations, that is 5.21 per cent, are held out. The measured station minus model height in the mountain class is a median of -178.3 m, which reproduces the 179 m figure this note quotes. Section 5 was done in full for the planned one-in-eight subsample in SLURM job 34859882 and cost about 80 seconds rather than the hours the note feared, because the five surface variables the head needs all sit inside the first 34-variable chunk of the target store, so one chunk read of about 0.9 GB serves a valid time rather than two; the raw observation minus analysis departure has a median of +0.075 K for 2 m temperature, +0.084 K for dewpoint, -0.324 m/s for wind speed and -12.6 Pa for mean sea level pressure.
